# Design principles (v2.0.0, Apple edition)

Seventeen principles drawn from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and WWDC design talks. Apple publishes no official list of 17, so this is a curated set.

1. Clarity: every label, icon and status says exactly one thing.
2. Deference: the UI steps back, the register data is the hero.
3. Depth: translucent header and blurred sheets show layers and context.
4. Hierarchy: big numbers, quiet headings, one primary action per screen.
5. Consistency: one radius, one spacing scale, one button language everywhere.
6. Feedback: hover, press and focus states respond to every touch.
7. Direct manipulation: rows, KPI cards and tabs are tapped directly.
8. Simplicity: borders and heavy chrome removed; whitespace does the work.
9. Aesthetic integrity: calm look suits a safety-critical tool; Shell yellow/red kept as accents.
10. User control: sheets can always be closed; nothing is forced.
11. Familiarity: segmented language control, tab bar, bottom sheets on phones.
12. Accessibility: 44 px targets, visible focus rings, high-contrast mode.
13. Typography: Jost, light uppercase page title (EcoOils standard), generous line height.
14. Meaningful colour: green/amber/red only mean OK/warning/expired.
15. Purposeful motion: short spring-like transitions; off for reduced-motion users.
16. Adaptivity: phone to wide monitor, safe-area insets, automatic dark mode.
17. Forgiveness: destructive actions keep their PIN and confirm steps.
